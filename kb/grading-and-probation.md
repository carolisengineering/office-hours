# Grading and Academic Probation

Courses are graded on a letter scale (A, A-, B+, B, B-, C+, C, C-, D, F). A grade
of **C- or higher** earns credit toward the degree. A grade below C- does not earn
credit, and the course must be retaken if it is required or a prerequisite.

## Cumulative GPA requirement

Students must maintain a **cumulative GPA of 3.0 or higher** to remain in good
standing and to graduate.

## Academic probation

A student whose cumulative GPA falls below 3.0 is placed on **academic probation
for one term**. During that term the student must:

- raise the cumulative GPA to 3.0 or above, and
- meet with their success advisor at least twice.

A student who does not return to a 3.0 cumulative GPA after the probation term is
subject to dismissal from the program. A single probation term does not appear on
the transcript; dismissal does.

Grades of "W" (withdrawal) do not affect GPA. See the Withdrawal and Refunds
document.
