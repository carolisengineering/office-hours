# Career Services

The Career Services team supports **currently enrolled students and program
alumni** at no cost. Services include:

- Resume and portfolio review for data-science roles.
- Mock technical and behavioral interviews.
- A job board of roles from employer partners.
- Salary-negotiation coaching.
- Introductions to the alumni network.

Career Services is available in every term, including terms a student is on an
approved leave of absence. It is **not** available to applicants who have not yet
enrolled.

Career Services does not guarantee job placement, does not sponsor work visas, and
does not provide immigration or legal advice. International students with
work-authorization questions should consult a licensed immigration attorney or
their employer's counsel.
