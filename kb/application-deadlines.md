# Application Deadlines

The Online M.S. in Data Science admits new students in all three terms. Application
deadlines are:

| Entry term | Application deadline |
|---|---|
| Fall | **July 15** |
| Spring | **November 15** |
| Summer | **March 15** |

All deadlines are 11:59 p.m. U.S. Central Time on the date listed. If a deadline
falls on a weekend or U.S. federal holiday, the effective deadline is 11:59 p.m.
Central on the next business day.

Applications submitted after a deadline are automatically rolled to the next term's
review cycle. There is no separate "late" or "priority" deadline, and there is no
application fee waiver deadline distinct from the dates above.

Admitted students receive an enrollment deposit request with their decision letter
and must confirm their intent to enroll within two weeks of the decision date.
