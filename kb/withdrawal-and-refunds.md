# Withdrawal and Refunds

Students may drop or withdraw from a course through their success advisor. The
tuition refund depends on when the withdrawal is processed, based on the 14-week
term:

| Withdrawal processed | Tuition refund | Transcript |
|---|---|---|
| Through end of **week 1** | **100%** | No record of the course |
| Weeks 2 - 3 (through end of week 3) | **50%** | No record of the course |
| From the start of **week 4** onward | **0%** | Grade of "W" |
| After the end of **week 10** | **0%** | "W" only with advisor approval; otherwise the student receives the earned letter grade |

The **technology fee is non-refundable** once the term begins.

A grade of "W" does not affect GPA and does not earn credit. Repeated withdrawals
from the same course (more than twice) require a meeting with the program director
before re-enrolling.

Federal financial aid may need to be partially returned when a student withdraws;
the Financial Aid Office calculates this separately and will contact the student.
