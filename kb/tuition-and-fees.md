# Tuition and Fees

Tuition for the Online M.S. in Data Science is **$925 per credit**. The program is
30 credits, so the total tuition cost is **$27,750** for students who complete the
program without repeating any courses.

## Fees

- **Technology fee:** $150 per term, charged in any term the student is enrolled in
  at least one course.
- There is no separate application fee for the M.S. in Data Science.
- There is no matriculation, graduation, or activity fee.

## Payment

Tuition and fees are billed at the start of each term and are due by the end of the
second week of the term. Students may pay in full or enroll in a term-based
installment plan through the Bursar's Office at no additional cost.

Riverton does **not** offer discounts, promotional codes, alumni discounts, or
referral credits for this program. Tuition is the same for in-state and
out-of-state students because the program is fully online.

Repeated courses are billed again at the standard per-credit rate.
