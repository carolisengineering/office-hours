# Admissions Requirements

To be considered for the Online M.S. in Data Science, applicants must submit:

- A **bachelor's degree** from a regionally accredited institution, in any field.
- An **undergraduate GPA of 3.0 or higher** (on a 4.0 scale). Applicants below 3.0
  may be admitted on academic probation at the admissions committee's discretion.
- Evidence of **one prior college course in statistics** and **one prior college
  course in programming** (Python preferred; any language accepted). These may be
  completed at any accredited institution, including community colleges and
  approved online providers, before the term begins.
- A **statement of purpose** (500-750 words).
- **Two letters of recommendation**, at least one from an academic or professional
  source who can speak to quantitative ability.

The **GRE is not required** and is not reviewed if submitted.

## Application review

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis after each deadline. Decisions are
typically released within four weeks of the deadline. The admissions committee does
not publish average accepted GPAs or acceptance rates, and the Office Hours
assistant cannot estimate an individual applicant's chance of admission.
