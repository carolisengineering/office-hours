# Advising and Student Outreach

Every student is assigned a **success advisor** at admission. The advisor is the
student's main non-academic contact for registration, prerequisites, transfer
credit, leave of absence, course-load changes, capstone registration, and referrals
to other offices.

## Proactive outreach

The program monitors engagement. If a student **misses two graded checkpoints in a
course without contacting the instructor**, the success advisor reaches out
directly to check in and connect the student with support. This is a supportive
process, not a disciplinary one.

## Meeting your advisor

Advisors hold weekly virtual drop-in hours and take appointments. Students should
contact their advisor **before registration closes** each term to confirm their
plan. Advisors do not assign grades, waive prerequisites, or override integrity or
accessibility decisions.

The Office Hours assistant can explain policies and point students to the right
office, but it cannot see individual student records such as grades, GPA, holds, or
registration status. For anything account-specific, contact your success advisor.
