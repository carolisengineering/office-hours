# DS 550: Data Ethics and Governance

**Credits:** 3
**Prerequisite:** DS 500

DS 550 covers fairness and bias in models, privacy (including differential
privacy), consent and data collection, transparency and the "right to explanation,"
dataset documentation, model documentation, regulatory frameworks, and building an
organizational governance process. The course is discussion-heavy and
reading-heavy; see the DS 550 Course Readings document for weekly summaries.

DS 550 is an elective and can be taken any time after DS 500. Many students take it
mid-program because its writing load pairs well with a lighter technical term.

Assessment: weekly reading responses, two case-analysis papers, and a final
governance proposal. No exam. Offered every term.
