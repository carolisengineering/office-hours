# Program Structure and Academic Calendar

The Online M.S. in Data Science at Riverton University requires **30 credits**,
completed as **10 courses of 3 credits each**. The program is fully online and
asynchronous, with optional weekly live sessions that are recorded for students who
cannot attend.

There is **no thesis**. All students complete a **capstone project** (DS 599) in
their final term.

## Terms

Riverton runs three terms per year: **Fall, Spring, and Summer**. Each term is
**14 weeks** long.

## Course load

- **Full-time:** 3 courses per term.
- **Part-time:** 1 or 2 courses per term.

Most part-time students finish in about **20 months**. Full-time students can
finish in about 12 months. Students may switch between full-time and part-time
from term to term by notifying their success advisor before registration closes.

## Enrollment status and financial aid

Federal financial aid requires at least **half-time enrollment**, which at Riverton
means **2 courses in a term**. Students taking a single course in a term are below
half-time and are not eligible for federal loans for that term. See the Financial
Aid document for details.
