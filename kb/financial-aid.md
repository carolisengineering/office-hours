# Financial Aid

U.S. citizens and eligible non-citizens can fund the Online M.S. in Data Science
with **federal financial aid**. To apply, file the **FAFSA** using Riverton
University's school code and list Data Science (Graduate) as the program.

## Federal loans

Graduate students enrolled at least **half-time** (defined as **2 courses in a
term** at Riverton) may borrow federal Direct Unsubsidized Loans and, if needed,
Grad PLUS loans up to the cost of attendance. Students enrolled in only one course
in a term are below half-time and are **not eligible for federal loans** for that
term.

## Institutional aid

Riverton does **not** offer merit scholarships, need-based grants, or assistantships
for this program. There is no institutional aid application.

## Employer reimbursement

Students whose employers reimburse tuition after the term ends may enroll in the
**deferred-payment plan**: the tuition due date is pushed to 30 days after grades
post, with proof of an employer tuition-assistance benefit on file. Contact the
Bursar's Office to set this up before the term starts.

The Office Hours assistant does not give personal financial, tax, or investment
advice.
