# Technology Requirements

Students need their own computer and internet connection. Minimum specifications:

- **Laptop or desktop less than 5 years old** (Windows, macOS, or Linux).
- **8 GB of RAM minimum; 16 GB recommended**, especially for DS 530 Deep Learning.
- **Broadband internet** capable of video calls (5 Mbps up / 10 Mbps down or
  better).
- A **webcam and microphone** for live sessions and the capstone presentation.

## Software

All required software is **free and open-source**: Python, Jupyter, Git,
PostgreSQL, PyTorch, and scikit-learn. The university provides free access to cloud
GPU notebooks for courses that need them, so a local GPU is not required.

There is no separate software or lab fee; the per-term technology fee covers
platform access. Students who cannot meet the hardware requirement should contact
their success advisor about the laptop loaner program before the term begins.
