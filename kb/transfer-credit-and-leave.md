# Transfer Credit and Leave of Absence

## Transfer credit

Students may transfer in up to **6 graduate credits** (two courses) toward the
30-credit degree. To be eligible, the coursework must be:

- from a regionally accredited graduate program,
- completed with a grade of **B or better**,
- not already applied to a completed degree,
- taken within the last **five years**, and
- approved by the student's success advisor as equivalent to a specific Riverton
  course.

Transfer credit can satisfy a prerequisite if the advisor maps it to the
corresponding DS course. DS 599 (Capstone) cannot be transferred.

## Leave of absence

Students in good standing may request a **leave of absence of up to 2 terms**
through their success advisor. Time on an approved leave does not count against any
time-to-completion limit. Students on leave keep their catalog year and do not
reapply. A leave longer than 2 consecutive terms requires readmission.
