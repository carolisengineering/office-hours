# DS 530: Deep Learning

**Credits:** 3
**Prerequisite:** DS 510

DS 530 covers neural network fundamentals: backpropagation, optimization,
regularization, convolutional networks for images, recurrent and attention-based
architectures for sequences, and transfer learning. Students train models in
PyTorch, using free cloud GPU notebooks where needed.

DS 530 is an elective; it is not required for the capstone. Students may take
DS 530 and DS 540 in either order once DS 510 is complete.

Assessment: three implementation checkpoints and a final project. Offered in Fall
and Spring only (not Summer).
