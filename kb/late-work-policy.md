# Late Work Policy

The program-wide default late-work policy is:

- **10% of the assignment's value deducted per calendar day late**, for up to
  **3 days**.
- After 3 days late, the assignment receives a **zero** and is no longer accepted.

Individual instructors may set a **more lenient** policy (for example, a longer
grace window or a fixed number of "no questions asked" extensions), but may not set
a stricter one than the program default. Each syllabus states the instructor's
policy.

## Extensions

Extensions beyond the instructor's stated policy are granted only for documented
emergencies (medical, family, or military) and are arranged through the success
advisor, not the instructor. Approved emergency extensions do not incur the daily
penalty.

Accommodations for disability-related extension needs are handled separately
through the Accessibility Office and are not subject to this policy.
