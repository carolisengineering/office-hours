# DS 599: Capstone

**Credits:** 3
**Prerequisites:** **24 completed credits**, which must include **DS 510** and
**DS 520**.

The capstone is a single term-long applied project: a student scopes a real
data-science problem, assembles data, builds and evaluates a solution, and delivers
a written report and a recorded presentation to a faculty panel. There is no thesis
and no separate comprehensive exam.

Because the capstone requires 24 credits plus two specific courses, it is almost
always the student's **final course**. A student who has 24 credits but has not
completed both DS 510 and DS 520 is not eligible and must take the missing course
first.

DS 599 is offered every term. Students register for it through their success
advisor rather than self-registration, so the advisor can confirm eligibility.
