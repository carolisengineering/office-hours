# Accessibility Services

Students with a disability may request academic accommodations through the
**Accessibility Office**.

## How to register

1. Submit a request through the Accessibility Office portal.
2. Provide **documentation** from a qualified professional describing the condition
   and its functional impact.
3. Meet with an access coordinator to design an accommodation plan.

Typical accommodations include extended time on assignments and assessments,
flexible checkpoint deadlines, alternative formats, and captioning for live
sessions.

## Timing

Accommodations take effect once the plan is approved and shared with instructors.
They are **not retroactive**: they do not change grades on work that was due before
the plan was in place. Students are encouraged to register as early as possible,
ideally before the term starts.

Accommodation decisions are made by the Accessibility Office and cannot be granted,
denied, or previewed by a success advisor, an instructor, or the Office Hours
assistant.
