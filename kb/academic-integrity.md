# Academic Integrity

Riverton's academic integrity policy prohibits plagiarism, unauthorized
collaboration, fabrication of data or results, contract cheating, and the
**unauthorized use of generative AI tools** on graded work.

## Generative AI

Each syllabus states whether and how AI tools may be used in that course. Unless a
syllabus explicitly permits it, students may not use AI tools to generate
submitted work, including application materials such as the statement of purpose.
When AI use is permitted, students must disclose it as directed by the instructor.

## Process and sanctions

A suspected violation is referred to the **Academic Integrity Board**. The student
is notified and may respond in writing and in a meeting.

- **First offense:** typically a course-level penalty (a zero on the assignment, or
  a failing grade in the course), plus a note in the student's non-transcript
  conduct file.
- **Second offense:** suspension or dismissal from the program.

Sanctions are decided by the Board, not by individual instructors or advisors, and
outcomes vary with the severity of the violation.
